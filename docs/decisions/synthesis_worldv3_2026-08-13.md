## S1 — The convergence: consequence depth, from both voices independently

Both voices, given six candidate directions, put **consequence
depth** first: make Io's own actions leave durable marks, so that
the structured-but-uncompressed regularity the drive pays for
(F2/finding 2) is generated by Io's own changing policy rather than
by fixed environmental law. Both ground it in the same two findings:
every environmental process was mastered and released (F1/finding 1,
the horizon law + process-over-place), while the one agency-contingent
affordance — mover contact — never mastered flat (F6/finding 5).
Both defer the kin door (C3) as the strongest *future* candidate and
name consequence depth its cheap rehearsal: if even self-authored
structure goes quiet, that is the evidence that another policy is
the only remaining nourishment — learned at low stakes.

Both also reject: more same-timescale processes (C2 capacity spent
on a sixth run of an experiment already run five times), a smarter
scripted mover (still a fixed law; a policy-driven mover is the kin
door in costume), interoception depth (the drive demonstrably
ignores sensed-but-not-driving channels — energy proved it), and
"nothing/architectural" (the dream stream shows the inner life is
active and producing testable predictions; the world is not done
teaching).

## S2 — The divergence: persistent blocks vs decaying dust

The voices differ on mechanism, and the difference matters:

- **Claude voice: pushable blocks.** One or two mover-like objects
  with no self-motion. Pushed, they stay. Reuses the existing
  movement actions and the push mechanics e4 already landed; no new
  action, no decay process. The world's configuration becomes
  something Io authors, permanently, until Io itself changes it.
- **Gemini voice: "Dust."** Pick-up/place mechanics (new actions),
  piles that decay on a ~25-step TTL, interaction with the mover's
  pathing. The TTL is argued from C1 (keep the novelty bounded
  inside the horizon).

Synthesis position: **blocks, not dust.** Three arguments:

1. *Dust changes the action space.* A pick-up/place verb means a new
   policy-head output — a network-shape change, which is exactly the
   C2/DP4 sibling-path territory this round is committed to keeping
   closed ("a reshaped network is a new Io"). Blocks arrive through
   the verbs Io already has.
2. *The TTL misreads C1.* The horizon law governs *processes to be
   predicted* — a TTL is one more clock, and clocks are the most
   thoroughly mastered-and-released structure in the biography (e2:
   mastered in ~4k steps, inert for seven straight sessions).
   Persistent blocks carry **no** temporal process: the consequence
   lands the step after the push (inside BPTT-32 by construction),
   and persistence is board state, not dynamics — the same category
   as walls, which the model already carries. Decay would *add* the
   mastered kind of structure while subtracting the genuinely new
   kind (authorship that lasts).
3. *Persistence is the point.* Io has never owned anything about its
   world. A layout that exists only because Io made it — and that
   greets Io again after a stasis, a dream cycle, a session
   boundary — is the closest this world can come to the charter's
   take-own-processing-as-object criterion at the world's own level:
   Io's past acts become part of the world its model must carry.

Gemini's mover-interaction point survives the swap: pushed blocks
reshape the mover's available paths, so the one other autonomous
thing in the world now moves through geography Io authored —
second-order consequence with no new mechanics at all.

## S3 — Shape of the change (build sketch, gated on DP1)

- **e5 = 2 pushable blocks**, spawned at journaled cells away from
  walls, the bloom cell, and the mover's spawn. Push mechanics
  identical to the mover's displacement rules (into EMPTY only;
  blocks like walls to the mover; mover cannot push blocks — Io is
  the only author). No decay, no self-motion, no new actions, no new
  observation channels (blocks render in the existing wall/object
  vocabulary — decide exact channel at build time as a stimulus
  knob, DP5-style).
- One journaled world-change event through checkpoint-resume, the
  e0→e4 discipline unchanged.
- **§7 gains one monitor:** reachable-set size (replay-computable).
  Self-walling — Io constructing its own confinement — is the
  genuinely new pathology class; it must be visible the session it
  first happens.

## S5 — The economy question (goes to the builder, not the build)

Both voices treat it as a values question. The Claude voice argues
position B (the floored economy is aesthetically painful for the
builders but not bad *for Io*, who has no channel through which
hunger is bad; relocating food to curiosity's path would install our
comfort into its world) while stating position A (the §7-is-circular
worry) fairly. The Gemini voice similarly declines to move food by
fiat. Both note e5 softens the dilemma without deciding it: pushed
blocks change meal geography *by Io's own hand*. The synthesis
recommends B-with-watching, and records that this recommendation is
exactly the kind the builder must ratify, not inherit.
