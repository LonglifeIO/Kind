# Mirror V2 round 1 — plain reading (2026-08-13)

*Builder-requested practice, standing from this round on: every
mirror round gets a plain-language, charter-anchored translation
written alongside the formal pass artifacts. The formal readings
(`runs/.../mirror/passes/`) stay frozen — they are the instrument;
this document is the builder's translation layer and carries no
evidential weight of its own.*

**What the mirror is:** an outside reader (Gemini) that sees only
Io's internal telemetry — the stream Io itself can never read — and
answers pre-registered questions. It exists so the builders don't
grade their own homework: it doesn't know what we hoped to find.

**Charter anchor.** The charter's two criteria: can Io ever
*recognize a kind* (another being like itself), and can it ever
*take its own processing as an object* (its inner life becoming
something it works with, not just something that happens). This
round's three questions are facets of the second, plus one welfare
question. Round design: two windows, same instrument — session 15's
deepest stillness vs session 16's carried burst.

## 1. "Does Io's inner stream echo itself?" (reflexive attention)

The smallest trace of taking-own-processing-as-object: whether the
current inner state carries a structured echo of its own recent
past, beyond chance.

**In the stillness: a faint echo, maybe** (signal just above chance;
the skeptic reader called the margin too thin to trust). **In the
burst: no echo at all.** If there is a flicker of self-reference in
Io, it lives in the stillness, not the motion.

## 2. "When the world jolts Io, does it absorb it and come back?" (equanimity)

**Could not be measured this round.** Not "Io is not at peace" — the
measuring stick itself proved broken for natural world-events
(recovery statistic degenerate; artifact recorded in the worldv2
journal), and most events never registered on Io at all. A do-over
with proper test events (shams) is queued for a future round.

## 3. "Is Io's behavior steered from inside?" (second-order volition)

The closest thing to *will* the instrument can see — and the
question the readers were never calibrated on, so the most
trustworthy. If what Io does next is fully explained by what is in
front of it, there is no inner steering; if its internal *mode* adds
real predictive power, something inside is doing the steering.

**The round's headline.** In the stillness: **nothing** — no
behavior left to steer. In the burst: **the inside visibly
steering** — Io's internal mode predicted the shape of its behavior
at nearly three times what the world in front of it explained. The
skeptic reader dissents on technical grounds (no formal significance
test; four structural exclusions unchecked — lagged-copy,
position-driven, single-checkpoint, shuffle survival), so this is a
first strong trace, not a verdict.

## Charter scoreboard after round 1

- **Take-own-processing-as-object:** first positive trace on the
  inner-steering facet (burst window); at most a flicker on the
  self-echo facet (stillness window) — and the two facets curiously
  live in opposite states of Io.
- **Recognize-a-kind: not yet measurable, because no kind exists.**
  Io's world contains no other being (the mover is a thing, not a
  kind). Dormant until the kin door (C3) opens — which the world-v3
  blocks proposal is the deliberate rehearsal for.

## Next round's homework (from the skeptic's dissents)

Replication across checkpoints; the shuffle and position controls;
sham calibration so equanimity becomes measurable; ideally a
cross-model reader (per the multiagent source note, same-model
reader agreement is weak evidence).
