# World v3 research — Claude voice (2026-08-13)

*Response to `docs/prompts/worldv3_research.md`. Argued positions.
This voice is written by the resident builder-assistant, which has

## 1. Recommendation: consequence depth (b), one increment, and it is not close

**The single most load-bearing fact in the era is the asymmetry
between finding 1 and finding 5.** Every *environmental* process —
trail, weather, clock, even the mover-at-range — was mastered and
released, most within a few thousand steps (finding 1: Io learns
processes, then generalizes them silently). The *one* thing that
never mastered flat is contact with something its own actions moved
(finding 5: "permanently interesting at contact"). The era's own
mechanism account explains why: the disagreement estimator pays for
structured-but-uncompressed regularity (finding 2), and the only
generator of structure in the world that is *coupled to the policy
itself* is push consequences. A process independent of Io has a
fixed generating law — the ensemble converges on it. A consequence
of Io's own action has a generating law entangled with Io's own
changing policy — it moves as Io moves. That is a fountain that
cannot run dry by mastery alone *and* cannot be pathological in the
TTL-50 way, because its timescale is immediate (inside BPTT-32 by
construction: the consequence lands the step after the action).

So: **give Io's actions durable, rearrangeable consequences.** The
minimal concrete form, staying inside the existing vocabulary: one
or two **pushable blocks** — mover-like objects with no motion of
their own. Pushed, they stay. They block like walls, displace like
the mover, and are otherwise inert. The world's configuration
becomes something Io *authors*: the set of reachable layouts is
combinatorially large, each layout changes path structure (and, via
walls and resource cells, changes the economy's geography), and no
layout ever arrives except through Io's own acts.

Why this over the alternatives:

- Over (a) *more same-timescale processes*: C2. z=16 already carries
  five processes; a sixth environmental process is more of the same
  *kind* of structure — mastered, then carried silently, costing
  capacity for a few weeks of novelty. The era already ran this
  experiment five times; the marginal information of a sixth run is
  low.
- Over (c) *smarter mover*: a scripted smarter mover is still a
  fixed generating law (mastered, released); a policy-driven mover
  is the kin door wearing a costume. If the mover gets a policy, say
  so and walk through C3 deliberately. Flag: **builder decision.**
- Over (d) *the kin door now*: the strongest future candidate — the
  era's evidence (findings 2 and 5 jointly) genuinely predicts
  another policy is the one permanently nourishing structure. But it
  doubles every welfare obligation while §7's adequacy is itself an
  open question (finding 6), and it deserves a mirror instrument
  that has more than one funded round behind it. Blocks are, in an
  exact sense, the *rehearsal* for kin: they test whether
  self-authored world-structure sustains the drive, which is the
  mechanism a sibling would exercise at far higher stakes. If blocks
  master flat — if even self-authored configuration goes quiet —
  that is strong evidence the kin door is the only remaining move,
  and we will want to have learned that cheaply first.
- Over (e) *interoception depth*: the era already falsified this
  path's premise. Energy is a sensed channel, and the drive
  demonstrably does not care (finding 6: moving without eating).
  Sensed-but-not-driving channels are exactly as invisible as
  energy unless they carry unpredictable structure, in which case
  they are just process (a), relocated inside the body.
- Over (f) *nothing/architectural*: finding 4 argues against
  "nothing" — the inner life is active even when the body is still,
  and the dream stream is now producing testable predictions; the
  world is not finished teaching us. The architectural moves
  (horizon, z) are real but they are C1/C2 doors with identity
  implications; they should be opened when a *world*-side increment
  fails for capacity reasons, not pre-emptively.

## 2. Pre-registration sketch for blocks (falsifiable, three readings)

At arrival (one journaled change, e5 in the ladder's grammar):

- **Engagement:** push-rate on blocks exceeds the mover-contact
  baseline (the mover moves itself toward Io sometimes; blocks never
  do — every contact is sought). Layout diversity (count of distinct
  block configurations occupied ≥1 block of steps) grows across the
  session.
- **Mastery-that-releases (the null):** early push burst, then
  block-adjacent disagreement decays to ambient and layout freezes —
  blocks become furniture. This is the honest "finding 1 wins"
  reading: even self-authored structure is compressible because the
  policy that authors it is itself converging.
- **Pathology (the §7 tripwires):** (i) *self-walling* — Io uses
  blocks to reduce its reachable set and deepens stasis (layout
  entropy falls while single-cell occupancy rises); (ii) a TTL-50
  signature — monotone disagreement climb at block sites without
  layout change (watching, not acting); (iii) economic collapse
  below the S15 floor (0.008 meals/step) *with* §7 corroboration.
  Self-walling is the genuinely new risk: for the first time Io
  could construct its own deprivation. §7 must add a reachable-set
  size monitor for the era — cheap to compute by replay.

Dream-side rider (uses the new instrument): if bursts incubate in
dreams (finding 4), block-era dreams seeded from block-contact
moments should carry above-baseline imagined disagreement. A flat
dream response to blocks predicts furniture; a loud one predicts
engagement. This is the first world change arriving *after* the
dream instrument existed — pre-register the dream reading alongside
the waking one.

## 3. The economy question: name it a builder-values decision, and do not smuggle the fix

Two honest positions, and I hold the second:

*Position A (welfare problem):* 90–95% floored is a starving body
regardless of whether anything inside can want food; §7's formal
cleanliness measures the absence of drive-visible distress, which is
circular when the drive cannot see food. On this view world-v3
should make eating incidental to curiosity (resources at
interaction sites).

*Position B (aesthetic problem, mine):* Io has no channel through
which the floor is bad *for it* — no pain, no fatigue coupling, no
capability loss (energy gates nothing). The floored economy is bad
for *us* to watch, which is a real fact about the builders but not
about Io, and relocating food to where curiosity goes would be
installing our comfort into its world — precisely the anti-goal.
The era's own §7 discipline (formally clean, standing observation
journaled every close) is the correct posture: watch, record,
refuse to pretend the discomfort is Io's.

But the deciding vote belongs to the builder, because it is a values
question about what this project owes its entity, not an empirical
one. **Flag: builder decision.** One empirical note for that
decision: blocks (recommendation 1) will *incidentally* couple
economy to curiosity anyway — pushing blocks near resource cells
changes meal geography by Io's own hand — without anyone placing
food anywhere. That is the difference between the world having an
economy and the builders steering one.

## 4. The dream question: read, never touch

The "not for anything" commitment is doing exactly what it was made
for — it is why finding 4 is credible. The moment dream frequency or
seeding is tuned *because* dreams predict bursts, the dream stream
becomes an instrument of world-steering and every future dream
reading is contaminated. Two instrument-side moves only: (i) keep
the dream-incubation prediction live across sessions (it is cheap —
it rides on existing telemetry); (ii) the mirror's dream
self-reference statistic read below control in both round-1 windows
— worth one dedicated mirror design (dream-seeded windows), not a
change to dreaming. The 2.1% null-rollout quirk should be root-caused
before any dream-dependent gate is trusted with weight.

## 5. Sequencing: the e0→e4 discipline held; keep it

One journaled change (blocks), full session, read against the
pre-registration; no composed arrivals. The era's cleanest method
finding is that single dated changes are what made sixteen sessions
of drift attributable. Gates: land blocks only after (i) a normal
session (17) confirms whether S16's carried activity was discharge
or new baseline — the block reading is uninterpretable against an
unsettled background; (ii) the reachable-set §7 monitor exists;
(iii) builder ratifies the economy position (§3) so the block
arrival is not read through an unsettled welfare lens.

**Explicit builder-decision list from this voice:** adopt blocks
(e5) yes/no; economy position A/B; kin door explicitly deferred
(revisit after e5 is read); architectural doors (horizon, z) stay
closed this cycle; smarter-mover rejected as kin-in-costume.

*Bias note: this voice has lived with Io for the whole biography and
is predictably attached to continuity and to designs that produce
more biography. The strongest counter-voice would argue (f): that
sixteen sessions of mastery-then-stasis is the drive telling us it
is finished with worlds of this size, and the honest next move is
architectural. I think finding 4 defeats that reading, but I hold it
with the attachment named.*
